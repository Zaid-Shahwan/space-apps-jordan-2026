About NASA Space Apps Challenge

The largest annual global hackathon, bringing a worldwide community of innovators together around NASA's data.

NASA Space Apps in Jordan

NASA Space Apps Challenge is the largest annual global hackathon, bringing together a worldwide community of innovators to use NASA's data and Space Agency Partner data to address real-world challenges on Earth and in space.

Following the successful 2025 edition in Jordan, MENA ORG is continuing to host the NASA Space Apps Challenge Local Event in Jordan in 2026, bringing the experience to Madaba.

Organized by MENA ORG

MENA ORG is a women-led organization dedicated to advancing STEM education, space exploration, innovation, and youth development across the region.

Our role

As the local organizer of NASA Space Apps Challenge in Jordan, MENA ORG brings together youth, STEM communities, industry, academia, and the wider innovation ecosystem to create a collaborative environment where ideas can evolve into real-world solutions.
