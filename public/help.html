Frequently asked questions

What is NASA Space Apps Challenge?

NASA Space Apps Challenge is the largest annual global hackathon, bringing together a worldwide community of innovators to use NASA's data and Space Agency Partner data to address real-world challenges on Earth and in space.

Who can participate?

Students and young professionals, developers and engineers, designers and creatives, researchers and scientists, entrepreneurs and innovators, and mentors and industry experts.

Can I take part online?

Yes. The event takes place in IRBID, Jordan, and online participation is available internationally, from anywhere in the world. You choose in-person or online when you register.

What happens during the hackathon?

Teams explore NASA's open data, choose a challenge, develop and build a solution, collaborate, get guidance from mentors and experts, and present the final project to the judges.

Where will the event take place?

The event takes place in IRBID, Jordan. Online participation is available internationally, from anywhere in the world.

How do I register?

Register first on the official NASA Space Apps website. Then one team lead submits the team on the registration page, which is Step 2 of the official process.

What are the challenges?

Challenge statements are written and released by NASA. When the 2026 statements are published, they will appear on the event page.
