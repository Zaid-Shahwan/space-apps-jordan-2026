Participation notes

This form is Step 2 of the official registration process. To participate, you must first register on the NASA Space Apps global site.

Open the global site (opens in a new tab)

  • Official registration first

    All participants must register first through the official NASA Space Apps website.

  • In person: IRBID

    In-person participants attend the event in IRBID, Jordan.

  • Online: worldwide

    Online participation is available internationally, from anywhere in the world.

  • No overnight stay

    No overnight accommodation is provided.

  • Transportation may be available

    Transportation coverage for participants traveling to IRBID may be available; however, availability is limited and cannot be guaranteed. Participants are encouraged to make their own arrangements accordingly.

  • Participants under 18

    Underage participants must have signed consent and a parent or guardian contact on file.

How registration works

Two stages. You need both to take part.

  1. 1

    Register on the official site

    Every participant creates a profile and registers on spaceappschallenge.org. This is required for everyone and is not replaced by this form.

  2. 2

    Register your team here

    One team lead completes the six short steps below for a team of one to six, including themselves.

Have this ready

  • Each team member's full name, email address and age group
  • A phone number the team leader can be reached on
  • Parent or guardian contact details if anyone on your team is under 18
