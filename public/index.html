The largest global hackathon, now in Jordan

NASA Space Apps Challenge is the largest annual global hackathon, bringing together a worldwide community of innovators to use NASA's data and Space Agency Partner data to address real-world challenges on Earth and in space.

MENA ORG is hosting the NASA Space Apps Challenge Local Event in Madaba, Jordan in 2026.

What happens at the hackathon

Two days, six stages, from open data to a finished project.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Explore NASA's open data and challenge themes.

  2. 02

    Ideate

    Choose a challenge and develop your idea.

  3. 03

    Build

    Build and refine your solution with your team.

  4. 04

    Collaborate

    Work with people from different backgrounds and skill sets.

  5. 05

    Get mentored

    Receive guidance from mentors and experts.

  6. 06

    Present

    Present your final project to the judges.
